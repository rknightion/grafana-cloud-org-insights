## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
collector/label_rules.json (versioned) plus loader with schema validation and a pure evaluate(stack_inventory, rules, tunables). Implements the frozen contracts in doc-0008 Part 1: result enum pass|fail|not_evaluated|not_applicable with closed reason enum (missing_input, route_parked, truncated, deadline, no_signal_data, precondition_false); one result per (rule, stack, signal) with closed-enum and numeric evidence only; truncation and byte-cap overflow semantics; applicable weight excludes judgement rules and parked or unapproved routes; severity low|medium|high weights 1/3/5 with PS band mapping; score = 100*passed/evaluated weight published only at coverage >= 0.8; catalogue_version; D-LBL8 size floor and static-infrastructure allowlist; rule-input registry extensible by E tasks.
